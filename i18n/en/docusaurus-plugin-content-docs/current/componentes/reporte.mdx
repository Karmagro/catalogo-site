## Description {/* #descripcion */}

An output component that extracts, processes, consolidates, and presents information from the system in a structured and meaningful format, oriented toward decision-making. Unlike a simple query, a report applies transformations (calculations, aggregations, summaries) to the data and presents them as a document with a specific purpose.

- Consolidates and processes data through calculations, aggregations, totals, averages, counts, or other analytical operations
- Presents the information in a structured format with defined sections (header, summary, body, conclusions)
- Generates the result as a document produced at a specific moment, not as a continuously updated view
- Has a specific purpose: answering business questions or concrete information needs

## Additional capabilities {/* #capacidades-adicionales */}

<div className={styles.capBlockOptional}>

<div className={styles.capHeading}>

  <span>Additional capabilities</span>

- **User parameterization:** lets the user adjust filters, date ranges, level of detail, or other parameters before generating the report
- **Export to multiple formats:** generates the result in downloadable formats (PDF, Excel, Word, HTML)
- **Scheduled generation and automatic distribution:** the system generates the report on a defined schedule and distributes it automatically to predefined recipients (by email or another channel)
- **Charts and visualizations:** incorporates tables, bar charts, trend lines, or other visual elements alongside the text

</div>

## Boundaries {/* #delimitaciones */}

<Admonition type="danger" title="What it is not">

- **Is not** [Queries](/docs/componentes/consultas): the Report consolidates, calculates, and aggregates information before presenting it; Queries deliver records exactly as they are stored
- **Is not** a [Dashboard](/docs/componentes/dashboard): the Report generates a document produced at a specific moment, designed to be exported and analyzed; the Dashboard is a continuously updated interactive view
- **Is not** a notification: notifications communicate specific events; reports present complete analyses of information
- **Does not** include capabilities for entering data into the system

</Admonition>

## Variants {/* #modalidades */}

- **Operational report:** details recent transactions and activities (e.g., an account statement)
- **Analytical report:** presents trends, comparisons, and more in-depth analysis
- **Regulatory report:** generated to meet regulatory or legal requirements, with predefined format and content
- **Executive report:** high-level summary oriented toward management decision-making

## Typical dependencies {/* #dependencias-tipicas */}

<div className={styles.dependencies}>

- [Database](/docs/componentes/base-datos) or central repository from which the data is extracted
- [Queries](/docs/componentes/consultas) that retrieve the necessary information
- [Authentication](/docs/componentes/formulario-login) and [Authorization](/docs/componentes/autorizacion) to control who can generate and view each report
- Messaging service ([Notification System](/docs/componentes/sistema-notificacion)) for automatic distribution, when that capability is present

</div>
